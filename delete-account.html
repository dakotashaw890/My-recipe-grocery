Whole Kitchen

Delete your Whole Kitchen account

You can delete your account here or in the app (Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete my account). Deleting permanently removes:

  • your sign-in (email and password)
  • your membership in your family. If you're its only member, the family's shared recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, pantry and photos are deleted too. Otherwise the other members keep them.
  • any recipes you shared to Community recipes, with their photos

Deletion happens right away and can't be undone. Copies kept on your own phone aren't touched; erase those in the app (Settings → Privacy & your data → Erase everything on this phone) or by uninstalling it. See the privacy policy.

Signed up with Google?

Forgot your password, or can't sign in? Email the address in the privacy policy from your account's email address and we'll delete it within 30 days.
