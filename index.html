Settings & Data

Backup files

A backup file (including your ingredient photos) protects you if a phone is lost. Keep one somewhere safe, like Google Drive or email.

Automatic backups

This phone saves a copy of your data about once a day, and before any big change (joining a family, importing, resetting). The last 10 are kept.

Prices

Prices you type in, or pay when you finish a trip, are used for cost estimates. Clearing them keeps package sizes and trip history.

Version 9 ingredient fixes

Only for kitchens started before version 9. Updates built-in recipes whose ingredients still match the old values; your own changes are kept. Download a backup first.
