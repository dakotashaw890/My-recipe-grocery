Packages, weights & prices

Ingredient package sizes

The usual package each ingredient is bought in. Shopping amounts are worked out from these.

Ingredient weights

How much a cup or one of each ingredient weighs, so recipe amounts turn into what you buy and nutrition adds up. Can be filled in from USDA measurements.

Prices

Prices you type in, or pay when you finish a trip, are used for cost estimates. Clearing them keeps package sizes and trip history.

Backups

Backup files

A backup file (including your ingredient photos) protects you if a phone is lost. Keep one somewhere safe, like Google Drive or email.

Automatic backups

This phone saves a copy of your data every day, plus a weekly copy, and before any big change (joining a family, importing, resetting). The last 10 daily copies and 8 weekly copies are kept, so you can go back about two months.
